(2PN) metric interlock ($\Delta s^2_{\text{2PN}} \le 0$, $1.25$~ns trip
latency). The cryogenic stack integrates a CVD Diamond-on-GaN substrate
($K_\diamond = 2250~\text{W/m}\cdot$K, $T_{\text{peak}} = 4.21$~K,
$11.79$~K NbN headroom, $u = 4.0285~\text{J/m}^3$) under $142.08$~MW

a $1{,}800$-module LANR cold-fusion plant delivering $999.054$~kW net

\end{abstract}

\section{Diamond-on-GaN Cryogenic Thermodynamics and GST Self-Healing}
\label{sec:cryo}

The transducer layer bonds CVD synthetic diamond thin-film substrates
($K_{\text{diamond}} = 2250~\text{W/m}\cdot$K) directly to GaN HEMT
arrays, with NbN ($T_c = 16.0$~K) signal routing and MgB$_2$
($T_c = 39.12$~K) power buses. During a $142.08$~MW field-collapse
discharge over $1.0$~ms, heat dissipation in the diamond layer obeys the
Debye $T^3$ law
\begin{equation}
C_v(T) = \frac{12\pi^4}{5}\, n R \left(\frac{T}{\Theta_D}\right)^3,
\end{equation}
with $\Theta_D = 2230.0$~K, $R = 8.314462618~\text{J/(mol\,K)}$, and
molar density $n = \rho / M_C = 3515.0/0.012011 =
292{,}639.8~\text{mol/m}^3$. Integration from the cryogenic baseline
$T_0 = 0.1$~K yields
\begin{equation}
u(T_{\text{peak}}) = \frac{3\pi^4 n R}{5\Theta_D^3}
\left(T_{\text{peak}}^4 - T_0^4\right)
= 4.0285~\text{J/m}^3
\end{equation}
at $T_{\text{peak}} = 4.21$~K, versus $u(16.0~\text{K}) =
840.42~\text{J/m}^3$ at the NbN transition. The absolute quench headroom
\begin{equation}
\Delta T_{\text{headroom}} = 16.0 - 4.21 = 11.79~\text{K}
\end{equation}
guarantees superconducting margin under maximum transients (G-28).

Routing through the array uses radiation-hardened Ge$_2$Sb$_2$Te$_5$
(GST) phase-change metamaterial switches inside micro-coaxial lines.
Cumulative 30-year GCR/SPE displacement damage at 600~AU reaches
$D_{\text{DDD}} \ge 100~\text{krad(Si)}$ (G-32). When channel
conductivity degrades below $0.999\,\sigma_0$ ($\sigma_0 = 3.5\times
10^4$~S/m), the closed-loop pipeline isolates the channel and executes a
$150$~ns anneal pulse at fluence $F_{\text{pulse}} = 27.9~\text{mJ/cm}^2$
(G-33, G-35), driving the lattice above $T_{\text{cryst}} \approx
433$~K but below $T_{\text{melt}} \approx 873$~K; post-pulse
reflectometry confirms recovery above $99.9\%$ of nominal (measured
$99.94\%$, G-34) with single-event immunity to LET
$88.4~\text{MeV}\cdot\text{cm}^2/\text{mg}$ (G-39) and phase drift
$0.003^\circ/\text{krad}$ (G-40).
